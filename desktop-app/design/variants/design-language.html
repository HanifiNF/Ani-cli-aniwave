What changes against the current app

  • Search pill becomes a true pill (12px → 22px corners) and grows into the palette in one surface; the palette used to be a separate panel hung under it.
  • Chips (segmented controls, source tags, genres, filter tokens) become pills; the chosen chip loses its drop shadow.
  • Corners settle on 10px (cards, posters, buttons, panels) and 8px (icon buttons, badges, checkboxes, keys); 5, 6, 7, 12, 14 and 16px go.
  • Weights: 600 becomes 700 on titles and 500 on labels. Letter spacing is −0.005em everywhere, so the tracked-out capitals (EP 28, FORMAT, STATUS…) become sentence-case 500 labels and the wordmark loses its tracking.
  • Monospace keys (⌘K, the hint pill) switch to Inter. Inter is bundled instead of relying on the system.
  • Flat fills: drop shadows go from the palette, panels, poster, the chosen chip and the corner player; a 12% hairline marks raised layers. The blurred badge backing becomes the background colour.
  • Scrim is the background at 72%, not black at 55%.
  • Highlight stays on Play, the keyboard selection, EP progress badges, ticked episodes, switches that are on and the today dot. The palette's current row gains a highlight ring to match the cards.
  • Icons keep their paths; the stroke is drawn in screen pixels so 13–18px icons share one line weight, and the filled play/pause glyphs in the corner player join the line set.
  • Motion: one spring engine replaces the cubic-bezier transitions and keyframes. The bell swing and the 1.18× count pop go (too elastic). Nav, chips and switches get two-edge indicators; the poster flies into the series page; Play grows into the player; the corner player follows the pointer and flings to a corner.
  • Kept as is: layouts, sizes, the grid, the T2 series header, the corner-player behaviour, Settings apply-on-change, the theme presets. Art masks (backdrop wash, ghost rows) stay: they are on artwork rather than chrome.
  • Open question: the source status dots use a fixed green and red, outside the three colours.
