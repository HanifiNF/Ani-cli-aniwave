**Ani Desktop UI and UX audit, 10 September 2026**

Reviewed the current local build through native computer use on macOS. The walkthrough covered home, search, empty results, provider failure, series details, a 500-episode series, saved titles, recent history and filtering, settings, custom colours, a light-theme preview, built-in playback, resume, player menus, shortcut help, fullscreen, the corner player, and compact window layouts.

The terminal-inspired visual style is coherent. The highest-value improvements are predictable search behavior, readable secondary information, better use of compact windows, and clearer navigation between the library, series, and player.

The user confirmed that AniDB is currently down. Its 503 response is an external service outage. Finding 02 concerns how the app presents that outage: stale selectable results and limited recovery guidance.

Priority meanings: **P1** causes an unexpected action or obscures the outcome of a core task; **P2** creates recurring friction; **P3** improves clarity and consistency. These findings describe observed interface behavior. Suggested solutions are design recommendations.

| 01 | P1 | Search and keyboard | After searching `zzzzpolishaudit`, the screen said `nothing found` and highlighted the first Continue title. | 10 | P2 | Settings organization and affordances | Playback, fullscreen, autoplay, diagnostic logging, executable path, quality, audio, theme, and provider URLs appear in one long sequence. Technical diagnostic settings come before everyday appearance preferences. Path and URL inputs blend into ordinary text. | 13 | P3 | Removal semantics and accessibility | Continue, Saved, and Recent use the same generic `remove` label. The native Help menu's Keyboard Shortcuts item was disabled while browsing the library, even though browsing relies on arrows, Enter, Escape, and search shortcuts. | Make shortcut help available throughout the app and include a browsing section. Retain concise, context-sensitive footer hints. |

**Recommended order of work**

1. Correct empty-search Enter behavior and provider-failure state handling.
2. Protect search width at compact sizes and improve secondary-text readability.
3. Preserve navigation context and separate opening details from starting playback.
4. Improve resume information and navigation within long episode lists.
5. Refine settings, metadata, labels, and shortcut discovery.

**Behaviors that worked well**

- Search returned working-provider results promptly during the walkthrough.
- Series details focused the resumable episode on entry.
- Built-in playback successfully resolved a stream and resumed the existing episode position.
- The player included labeled seek controls, playback settings, and a keyboard shortcut dialog.
- Native fullscreen settled into a clean video view with navigation chrome hidden.
- The corner player retained the title, episode, timestamp, play/pause, expand, and stop controls.
- Theme selection previewed immediately, and cancelling restored the original Nord theme.
- Recent filtering provided a specific empty-state explanation.

**Coverage and session notes**

This was an interactive product audit, rather than an exhaustive test of every state. AniDB's live 503 prevented inspection of successful AniDB playback and multi-provider episode results. External-player launching, picture-in-picture, episode-completion/autoplay, destructive history actions, first-run empty libraries, every theme, and other operating systems were not exercised. Fatal-player recovery was not deliberately induced. Accessibility observations came from the exposed native accessibility tree and keyboard interactions, rather than a complete screen-reader audit.

The app was left on home with playback stopped, AniWave selected, and the original Nord theme restored. The existing Frieren resume position and recent-use timestamp advanced slightly during playback checks. Settings previews were cancelled. Application source files were left unchanged; this report is the sole repository addition.
